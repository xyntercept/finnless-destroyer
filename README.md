# Finnless Destroyer
This is a mod for Cookie Clicker that enhances the experience of seed scouting on the [Finnless ruleset](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qgqDVmOy3aTUjA0LTQnM3GXf6R9bXU7GU_ufSDC7h4o/edit?gid=1814447092#gid=1814447092). It uses a more advanced combo-finding algorithm than the Combo Finder in FtHoF planner, and it's also a mod that works similar to Sweet Finder, so you don't even have to check planner every time you ascend. The mod checks for combos on its own, according to your settings.

That being said,

### This mod is NOT worth using if you have less than 1 trigintillion cookies baked. Please do not use it until you have reached that point. It is not worth it.
That isn't shooting for trigintillions in the next combo, that is 1 trigintillion cookies baked already.

Specifically, the quality of combos smaller than decacasts may not be very high. This mod mainly searches for advanced yet rewarding techniques, such as GFD offset abuse, which are not at all worth the trouble until you have a lot of cookies. This mod also does not count regular FtHoF casts when finding combos, since they're typically used far less than GFD FtHoFs in large routes. Since even something like a novcast could have as few as two GFD FtHoFs, the mod may leave many easy-to-execute novcasts completely undetected. And if you want to do the large combos earlier than most, that's not worth it either. Save those for when you have a higher PL and are more skilled, and you'll get so much more out of them, and get less demotivated.

More information about the mod is available [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RbWVE4juszGuLC83EaBXj3FiqinHLMdXNpgwtT2Futc/edit?tab=t.0). If you have any feedback, DM @xyntercept on Discord.
